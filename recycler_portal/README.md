# @ecobridge/recycler-portal 🏭

The **Recycler Web Portal** is an operations portal built with Next.js for verified recycling plants, dismantling facilities, and Producer Responsibility Organizations (PROs).

## Key Capabilities
- **Gate Inwarding:** Fast QR / barcode scanning of arriving collector batches.
- **Weighbridge Integration:** Direct reading and reconciliation of scale weights against collector-reported logs.
- **Grading & Dispute Management:** Step-by-step verification of material grades (Grade A/B/C) with photo documentation.
- **Escrow Approval:** Authorizes instant automated payouts upon mutual lot acceptance.
- **EPR Certificate Generation:** Generates auditable destruction and recycling manifests for compliance reporting.
